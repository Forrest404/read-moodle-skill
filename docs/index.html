Setting up

Claude does the installing. You only do three things.

  1. Log in to MoodleYour browser opens Moodle. Log in as usual, then click Open when it asks to open MoodleKey.
  2. Pick your modulesChoose them from a list and confirm your term dates.
  3. Restart Claude CodeThe commands appear. Connecting Notion is optional.

What you can ask

Type a command, or just ask in normal words, like "what do I need to read for Thursday?"

/today
A short daily briefing: what's due, what to do today, this week's sessions and readings, new announcements.
/brief <assignment>
Reads the brief, attachments and marking criteria, and tells you what you actually have to do.
/deadlines [days]
Everything due in the next 14 days, submitted or not.
/plan
Breaks your deadlines into steps and adds them to your calendar. It asks before adding anything.
/module <code> [week]
A module's week: sessions, slides, readings, announcements.
/grades [module]
Grades, with a short summary of written feedback.
/assignments [module]
All assignments with due dates, status and grades.
/ask <question>
Anything else on Moodle.

What it can see and change

It reads Moodle the same way the official Moodle app does, using your own key.

Reads

Your courses, deadlines, assignment briefs and files, week pages and readings, announcements, submission status, grades and feedback.

Never

Submits, uploads, posts, replies or changes settings on Moodle. Any Moodle function that could change something is refused. It never asks for or sees your password.

Adds to your Mac

  • ~/read-moodle-skill, with your settings and downloaded briefs
  • the commands, in ~/.claude/skills
  • MoodleKey.app, a small helper built on your Mac
  • your Moodle key, in Keychain

Only if you choose: a Notion page for /today, a Notion database for /plan, and calendar events you import. No servers, accounts or tracking. Moodle data goes to Claude only when you ask something.

Help

If something breaks

Run python3 -I ~/read-moodle-skill/moodle.py doctor. It checks each part and says how to fix it.

If your key stops working, for example after a password change, run the same command with login instead of doctor.

Update or remove

Update: cd ~/read-moodle-skill && git pull && ./install.sh

Remove: ~/read-moodle-skill/uninstall.sh && rm -rf ~/read-moodle-skill
