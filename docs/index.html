Moodle, minus the clicking.

A few commands for Claude Code that read your Moodle for you. Ask what's due, what a brief actually wants, or what to read before Thursday, and get a straight answer with links.

Mac and Claude Code. Claude sets it up; you log in to Moodle once.

What you can ask

Eight commands, or just ask normally.

I wrote these because I was opening four module pages every week to piece together what was due and what to read. Now it's one line. You don't have to remember the commands either: "what do I need to read before the seminar?" works fine.

  • /todayWhat's due, this week's sessions and readings, new announcements.
  • /deadlinesEverything due in the next two weeks, and whether it's submitted.
  • /brief <name>The brief and marking criteria, read for you.
  • /module <code> week 7One module's week, with slides and readings.
  • /gradesYour marks and what the feedback says.
  • /planDeadlines broken into steps, added to your calendar after you OK them.
  • /assignmentsEvery assignment, with dates and status.
  • /ask <anything>For everything else on Moodle.
How it works

The same door the Moodle app uses.

Moodle has an API for its mobile app. During setup you log in once in your browser and a tiny helper catches the key Moodle hands back. From then on, a small script on your Mac asks Moodle for your deadlines, pages and files, and Claude turns the answer into something readable.

Moodleyour courses, briefs, grades
→
Your Macmoodle.py + your key in Keychain
→
Claude Codereads it and answers you

It never sees your password, and the script refuses anything that isn't a read. That's enforced in the code, not just written in the instructions.

What it touches

Exactly what it can see and change.

Reads from Moodle

  • Courses and week pages
  • Deadlines and submission status
  • Briefs, slides and files
  • Announcements
  • Grades and feedback

Never does

  • Submit or upload
  • Post or reply
  • Mark anything as done
  • Change settings
  • Ask for your password

Adds to your Mac

  • ~/read-moodle-skill
  • Commands in ~/.claude/skills
  • MoodleKey.app, built locally
  • Your Moodle key, in Keychain
  • Notion, only if you connect it
Small print

Worth knowing before you install.

  1. I've only tested it on UCL Moodle. Other Moodle sites may work if they allow the Moodle app.
  2. Mac only, for now.
  3. If UCL turns off access for the Moodle app, this stops working too.
  4. Coursework handed in on Turnitin or similar shows as "not submitted" on Moodle. It'll tell you that rather than panic you.
  5. The code is short and it's all on GitHub. Have a read before you run it.
Help

If something stops working.

Check it

python3 -I ~/read-moodle-skill/moodle.py doctor checks every part and tells you what to fix. After a password change, run it with login instead.

Update or remove

Update with git pull && ./install.sh in the folder. Remove with ./uninstall.sh, then delete the folder.

Found a bug? Open an issue.
